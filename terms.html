1. The Service

Tableau Events is a web-based wedding planning platform for engaged couples in South Africa. We offer a free plan and one paid plan, Premium:

PlanPriceWhat's included
Free PlanFreeGuest list, table creation & seating, floor plan design, QR code for guests
PremiumR299 once-offEverything in Free, plus the full guest page (programme, menu, a note from you, gifts and banking details, guest photo album, song requests, links and Canva template sets) for up to 4 events, such as your wedding, engagement party, kitchen tea and rehearsal dinner
