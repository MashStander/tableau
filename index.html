FAQ

Good to know

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the page opens in their browser. If they want, they can save it to their home screen.

What if I change the seating after I’ve printed the QR code?

That’s fine. The QR code never changes. Your changes go to your guests by themselves within seconds.

Is it in Afrikaans?

Your guest page can be in English or Afrikaans, or in your own words for any other language. The planning side, where you work, is in English for now.

Can I do it all on my phone?

Yes, you can add guests and seat them on your phone. The floor plan is easiest on a computer, because of the bigger screen.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. You pay R299 once. It covers up to 4 events and you can edit for 18 months. Your guest pages stay online afterwards.

Who can see my guest page?

Only people with your QR code or link. Your guest page doesn’t show up in Google.

Why we built Tableau

A note from a mom who’s been there

Just over a year ago, Erik and I attended five back-to-back weddings of young people who practically grew up in our home—watching the planning from the sidelines was both a thrill and a workout. This year, we planned two of our own: my son’s and my daughter’s (who married one of her brother’s best mates). Two weddings in one year! Seeing it all up close taught me how quickly the joy gets buried under seating charts, frantic WhatsApp groups, and details you spend hours creating for a five-second glance from guests. I built Tableau to trade the planning chaos for a little more magic—taking the stress off you beforehand, and making every guest, from varsity friends to Ouma, feel welcomed in their own language. Think of it as a note from a mom who’s been right in the thick of it: pour the champagne and go enjoy your day.

Mash & Erik
